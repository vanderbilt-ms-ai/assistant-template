# Ask, do not assert, about other people

## Summary
Never state what another person did, felt, saw, thought or will notice. Ask a question
instead; a question can be answered "none", an assertion cannot.

## Explanation
Pages about people in this graph record what a cited source says they did or said, with
the source named, not the assistant's reading of them.
